## Coverage and Performance Evidence

The workload artifact reports 30 samples for each isolated database case. It records baseline/guarded checkout and replay p50/p95, denied attempts, order identity/ownership/amount invariants and client request counts. In its visible 30s / hidden 60s / visible 30s status scenario, requests fell from 41 to 22 and hidden polls from 20 to 0. A 20-event focus burst fell from 20 router refreshes to 1, with zero extra session fetches in both runs. These are local/fake-clock observations; they do not establish production network latency, provider throughput or capacity.

No coverage percentage report was generated by the plan gates. Critical auth, SQL policy/concurrency, route and polling paths have targeted tests; hosted Supabase parity and controlled browser presentation remain unverified.

The reviewer found no blocking correctness or payment-safety issue and approved with concerns. Remaining performance evidence gaps: (1) the `order_code` partial index for the event anti-join is definition-checked but not EXPLAINed or stress-tested against a large event ledger; (2) the 20,003-order EXPLAIN fixture places most rows under a different account and asserts only that the order index name appears, so it does not establish high-volume single-account behavior, the precise index condition, or an upper-range bound for future-dated rows. The effective upper bound remains a per-account residual `least(...) <= now` filter.
